## Section: What we do
Heavy, awkward or easy to damage? That's the kind of load we deal with. AOE Engineering supplies the lifting equipment and takes repetitive handling off your people.

We design manipulators, build them, install them and commission them. If your workshop needs changing to take the new machinery, we do that too. You don't have to organise any of it.

We're the UK agent for ATIS and supply its manipulators. We also supply manipulators and equipment from other makers, and pick whatever suits your process.

## Section: Honest about automation
Automation isn't right for every company. If it isn't right for yours, we'll say so.

Where it would make a serious difference, we lay out the options and show you which one gives the best return. Then it's your call.

We'd rather work with you for years than sell you one machine.

## Section: Manipulators
ATIS makes four families of pneumatic manipulator. ATISmirus has a rigid arm. ATISacer runs on a double rope, ATISferax works on a lever, and ATISlinear moves up and down on linear guides. Between them they lift from 55 kg to 1,000 kg.

Button: See the manipulators

## Section: Gripping
The arm is only half of it. What holds the load matters just as much: mechanical clamps, pneumatic clamps, magnets, forks, hooks or vacuum cups. The right one depends on what you're lifting.
