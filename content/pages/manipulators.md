# Industrial manipulators

## Section: Intro
Four families. All pneumatic. All made by ATIS: ATISmirus, ATISacer, ATISferax and ATISlinear. Between them they lift anything from 55 kg to 1,000 kg.

We're the UK agent for ATIS, so we supply them. We install and commission them, and keep them serviced after. We also design and build manipulators ourselves.

## Section: ATISmirus: rigid arm
The rigid arm. Six models, from 115 kg right up to 1,000 kg, which makes it the widest spread of capacity in the range.

| Model | Max capacity | Radius | Vertical stroke |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATISmirus 80 | 115 kg | 3,100 mm | 1,500 mm |
| ATISmirus 100 | 180 kg | 4,000 mm | 2,250 mm |
| ATISmirus 200 | 260 kg | 3,250 mm | 2,250 mm |
| ATISmirus 300 | 440 kg | 4,400 mm | 2,250 mm |
| ATISmirus 500 | 600 kg | 4,000 mm | 2,100 mm |
| ATISmirus 800 | 1,000 kg | 4,200 mm | 2,250 mm |

What does that look like on the floor? ATIS has set up ATISmirus arms to tilt bushings, move boilers off a conveyor onto a trolley, stack taped boxes on a pallet three at a time and feed metal sheets into a machine.

Take the ATISmirus 200 VT, set up for windows. It picks each window out of its container and puts it down on a forklift. Suction cups adjust for different sizes and thicknesses. A brake holds the load still while the operator grips and lets go. And if the vacuum drops suddenly, safety valves keep hold of the window.

## Section: ATISacer: double rope
Double rope. Three models, 55 kg to 150 kg.

| Model | Max capacity | Radius | Vertical stroke |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATISacer 55 | 55 kg | 4,000 mm | 1,600 mm |
| ATISacer 80 | 95 kg | 4,100 mm | 1,800 mm |
| ATISacer 150 | 150 kg | 5,000 mm | 1,800 mm |

ATIS has used an ATISacer 55 with a shaped hook to lift paint drums off a production line and onto a pallet.

## Section: ATISferax: lever
The lever manipulator. Just the one model: up to 80 kg, 4,000 mm of reach and 1,500 mm of vertical stroke. ATIS describes it as low inertia.

## Section: ATISlinear: linear guides
Straight up and down on linear guides, hung from an overhead rail. Up to 800 kg.

ATIS has run an ATISlinear on a runway with a pneumatic clamp to carry frames from a production line to a bench.

## Section: How we help
Not every handling job needs a manipulator. If yours doesn't, we'll tell you.

Where one would make a serious difference, we look at the options for your process, put together concept designs and show you which gives the best return. That might be ATIS. It might be another maker's equipment. Whatever suits the job.

Then we get on with it. Design, build, installation, commissioning. If your workshop needs changing to take the new machinery, we do that too, and you don't have to organise any of it.

After that, we service manipulators and lifting equipment of every make, ATIS or not.

For kit we supply, we'll put together a support package around you: scheduled maintenance, statutory inspections and training for your team.
