# Solutions

## Section: Intro
Most handling problems come down to the load. What it weighs. What shape it is. How easily it marks. Here are the jobs ATIS manipulators are set up for, load by load.

If yours isn't here, ask us anyway.

## Section: Window frames, doors and gates
Aluminium, PVC and timber profiles, lifted and placed without the operator taking the weight.
Image: frames-windows | ATISmirus 300 with a suction gripper lifting a window frame

## Section: Glass
Large panes on vacuum cups: float glass, safety glass and insulating units. The ATISmirus 200 VT takes a window from its container to a forklift, with cups that adjust to the size and thickness.

## Section: Sheet metal
Sheets lifted and positioned on stamping, cutting and assembly lines. An ATISmirus 300 with adjustable suction cups picks sheets from a pallet, the floor or a shelf and feeds them into a machine.
Image: sheets-mirus300 | ATISmirus 300 with adjustable suction cups lifting a metal sheet

## Section: Steel plates, tubes and profiles
Ferrous loads held on magnetic gripping tools.
Image: plates-mirus200 | ATISmirus 200 with a gripper for metal plates

## Section: Cardboard boxes
Palletising and depalletising at the end of the line, where the cycles are high. An ATISmirus 200 with multiple suction cups moves taped boxes three at a time from a roller conveyor onto a pallet.
Image: boxes-mirus200 | ATISmirus 200 with multiple suction cups lifting taped cardboard boxes

## Section: Bags and sacks
Flexible loads like chemical powders, cement and food sacks, handled without splitting them.
Image: sacks-acer150 | ATISacer 150 with a suction gripper lifting a sack

## Section: Drums
200-litre drums lifted, tilted and turned. Used in chemical, paint and food plants. An ATISacer 55 with a shaped hook moves paint drums off a production line onto a pallet.
Image: drums-mirus300 | ATISmirus 300 with a clamp gripping a drum

## Section: Coils
Heavy paper, plastic and metal coils, gripped on the core or the outside diameter with a pneumatic clamp.
Image: coil-magnet | ATISmirus 100 with a double magnet gripping a roll of metal strip

## Section: Boilers, bushings and frames
Boilers moved from a roller conveyor to an assembly trolley on covered forks. Bushings gripped upright or on their side and tilted. Frames carried overhead from the production line to a bench on an ATISlinear.
Image: bushings-pneumatic | ATISmirus 100 with a tilting pneumatic gripper for bushings

## Section: Is it worth it?
Not always. Automation isn't right for every company, and we'll tell you if it isn't right for yours. Where it would make a serious difference, we'll show you the options and which one gives the best return.
