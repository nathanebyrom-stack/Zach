About

AOE Engineering is a team of engineers. We move loads that are heavy, awkward or easy to damage. Our background is in production equipment design, on the front line of manufacturing. We solve technical manufacturing problems and deliver equipment that works for demanding customers.

What we do

We design and build manipulators, then install and commission them. We're the UK agent for ATIS, and we also supply manipulators and equipment from other makers. We adapt your workshop to take the new machinery, and we service lifting equipment of every make.

How we work

Automation isn't right for every company. We only recommend it where it makes a serious difference, then show you the options that give the best return.

We'd rather work with you for years than sell you one machine.

What we've built

  • Bespoke automotive manipulators
  • Fully automated conveyor systems
  • Gantry cranes
  • Industrial steel structures
  • Military and civil aerospace process equipment
  • Offshore and subsea handling equipment
  • Special-purpose industrial machinery
