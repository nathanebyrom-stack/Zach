How we start

Most handling problems aren't solved by buying a machine. They're solved by understanding the load, the people who move it and the process around it.

That's where we begin. We look at what you lift, how often and where. Then we recommend the equipment that fits, and say so plainly when something simpler would do.

Services

From first visit to aftercare

One team, start to finish.

We're a 360 company. We cover design, installation, workshop adaptations and servicing, so you deal with one team throughout.

  1. Understand

    We see the load and the task for ourselves. Weight, shape, surface, cycle and the space around it.

  2. Design

    We specify the lifting equipment and gripping tooling, and adapt the workshop where needed.

  3. Install

    We fit the equipment on site and make sure it works the way the design intended.

  4. Support

    We service and maintain it for the long term, along with any other manipulators you run.

Honest about automation

Automation isn't right for everyone.

We only recommend automating a task when there's a serious benefit in doing it. If there isn't, we'll tell you.

When there is, we set out the options side by side and show you which gives the best return. You decide with the full picture in front of you.
