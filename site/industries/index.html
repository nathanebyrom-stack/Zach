A / Automotive

Automotive

The same lift, many times a shift.

Automotive work is about repetition. A component that's manageable once becomes a strain injury risk by the hundredth lift. Parts are often heavy for their size, and many have finished surfaces that can't be marked.

We look at the cycle as a whole: where the part comes from, where it goes and how the operator moves between the two. Then we decide whether a manipulator, a different gripper or automation gives the best return.

  • Wheels and tyres
  • Body panels
  • Engine and gearbox parts
  • Glazing

B / Defence

Defence

High-value parts, placed exactly.

In defence the cost of a dropped or knocked part is high. Loads tend to be heavy, dense and valuable, and they often need placing with care in tight spaces.

Control matters more than speed here. We design handling that holds the load securely and lets the operator position it precisely, and that fits the way your site already works.

  • Heavy, dense components
  • Precision assemblies
  • Loads in confined spaces

C / Windows & Doors

Windows & Doors

Big, heavy and easy to break.

Glazed units and doors are large, awkward to grip and unforgiving if they flex or knock. Most have finished faces and edges that must arrive unmarked.

The gripper does most of the work here. We match the tooling to the product and the way it needs turning or tilting, so one operator can move it without risking the glass or themselves.

  • Sealed glazed units
  • Window frames
  • Doors and door sets

D / Wine

Wine

Heavy work at harvest, and every day after.

Wine production mixes heavy, rounded loads with fragile glass. Barrels are awkward to hold. Cases and bottles are lifted again and again. Floors can be wet and spaces tight.

We look at where the manual handling is heaviest and most repetitive, and we only suggest equipment where it clearly earns its place.

  • Barrels
  • Cases
  • Bottles
