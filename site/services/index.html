Services

Selling a machine is the easy bit. Getting it into your workshop and keeping it running is the real work, and we do the lot. Process review, design, supply, fitting, workshop changes, servicing. One company, start to finish.

Feasibility and concept design

We look at the options for your process and turn them round into quick concept designs. You see the technical benefits laid out plainly, our team presents the options to yours, and the programme carries less risk from day one.

And if automation isn't the answer, we'll say so.

Design and build

Short of capacity, or the right skills in-house? We'll source and manage the manufacture and assembly of custom components and equipment for you. Ask us for a quote.

We design and build manipulators too, then install and commission them. We're the UK agent for ATIS, and we supply manipulators and equipment from other makers where they suit the job better.

Workshop adaptations

New machinery often means changes to the workshop. We make them. You don't have to organise any of it.

Project management

We coordinate the partners and suppliers, manage the risk and work to hit the date and the budget. We run our own equipment projects this way, and you can hire us to do the same on yours.

Servicing, all makes

We service manipulators and lifting equipment of every make. ATIS or anyone else's.

Equipment support

For equipment we supply, we'll put together a care package around you. It can cover scheduled maintenance, statutory inspections and training for your team.
